## Selected Projects

**[Live Betting Strategy for Soccer](https://github.com/EfeBuyukarslan/Live-Betting-Strategy-for-Soccer)**
In-play match outcome prediction on live match data. Feature engineering from evolving game state, Random Forest feature selection, and decision tree / XGBoost / SVM classifiers with randomized hyperparameter search, evaluated as a betting strategy on held-out matches.

**[Betting Odds Analysis and Match Outcome Prediction](https://github.com/EfeBuyukarslan/Betting-Odds-Analysis-and-Soccer-Match-Outcome-Prediction)**
Converting bookmaker odds into implied probabilities, removing the bookmaker's margin, and comparing implied against realised outcome frequencies to find where the market is mispriced. Includes noise filtering for late goals and early red cards, and decision trees fitted with and without odds features to assess market efficiency.

**[Event-Driven Analysis of Soccer Game Dynamics](https://github.com/EfeBuyukarslan/Event-Driven-Analysis-of-Soccer-Game-Dynamics)**
Statistical process control applied to live match metrics: rolling 15-minute control charts with 3-sigma limits to detect shifts in team attacking rates, then testing whether in-game events explain the detected out-of-control points.

**[Solar Energy Production Forecasting](https://github.com/EfeBuyukarslan/Solar-Energy-Production-Forecasting)**
Hourly solar plant output forecasting from weather variables in R. KPSS stationarity testing, differencing, ACF/PACF order selection, and ARIMA versus linear regression compared on BIC and residual diagnostics.
